Camera problem

Position your face: sit so your face is inside the square in the camera preview. The square turns green when both eyes are detected. Stay in that position, with your head still, for the whole test.
Calibration: keep your head still and your eyes fixed on each dot while you click it 5×. A dot disappears when it is done.
Check: follow the dot with your eyes as it moves, without moving your head. This takes about a minute.
